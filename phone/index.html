How positions are recorded

The selected marker supplies a known floor coordinate. All boxes scanned at that marker are assigned to its location; the phone does not measure their exact positions. Scan a new marker whenever you move.

A truck marker records collection only after you scan the box label. Scan loaded boxes, not boxes still awaiting pickup.

Photos are processed on the computer running PackTrack and are not saved. Keep the page open if you need to retry a failed scan.
